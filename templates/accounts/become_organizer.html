What it means to be an Organizer

Organizers are trusted members of the EYTGaming community who create and manage tournaments, list venues, and run esports events. Each application is reviewed to ensure quality and reliability.

✓ Create and manage tournaments with custom brackets
✓ List gaming venues and accept bookings
✓ Collect entry fees and manage prize pools
✓ Full analytics dashboard for your events
✓ Verified organizer badge on your profile
